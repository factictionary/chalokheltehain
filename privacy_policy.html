Privacy Policy for Chalo Khelte Hain

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Chalo Khelte Hain ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application ("App") and related services, and explains how you can exercise your rights, including requesting the deletion of your account and associated data.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Display name, registered phone number, and preferred sports roles submitted during Google Sign-In or Phone OTP authentication.
  • Match & Event Details: Game schedules, venue locations, attendance roll-calls, team compositions, and expense records.
  • Settlement Handles: Optional UPI IDs / Virtual Payment Addresses provided by organizers to facilitate peer-to-peer settlement outside the app. (Note: We do not process banking transactions, store credit cards, or collect financial credentials.)

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, crash reports, and network connectivity state.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) and Apple IDFA for non-personalized and personalized advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Facilitate recreational sports match scheduling, RSVPs, waitlists, and attendance tracking.
  • Generate formatted WhatsApp roll-call lists and payment settlement summaries upon user request.
  • Calculate fair per-head costs and manage equipment kitty balances.
  • Display in-app advertisements (banner, interstitial, rewarded) via Google Mobile Ads.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising Mediation

We work with trusted third-party providers:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication and Cloud Firestore database (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob: Primary ad delivery network (AdMob Privacy).
  • Unity Ads (Mediation Adapter): Ad network partner (Unity Privacy).
  • Liftoff Monetize / Vungle (Mediation Adapter): Ad network partner (Liftoff Privacy).

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Information is only shared within your match groups (e.g., rosters shown to teammates) or when legally required by law. All data is protected using industry-standard Google Cloud Firebase security protocols.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention Mechanism

In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users have full control over their account data and may request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal records at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account using either of the two methods below:

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Immediate Self-Service)

  1. Open the Chalo Khelte Hain app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the top right corner of the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the profile screen and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Review the confirmation dialog and confirm. Your account and personal records will be deleted immediately.

Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request (Without installing the App)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request account deletion online without needing to reinstall:

  • Send an email to factictionary@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Chalo Khelte Hain
  • Required Information: Include your registered phone number or Google Sign-In email associated with your account so we can verify your identity.
  • Processing Timeline: Web/Email deletion requests are verified and processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once completed.

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately to have it purged.
