Privacy Policy for Chalo Khelte Hain

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Chalo Khelte Hain ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application ("App") and related services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Display name, phone number, and preferred sports roles submitted during Google or Phone OTP authentication.
  • Match & Event Details: Game schedules, venue locations, attendance roll-calls, team compositions, and expense records.
  • Settlement Handles: Optional UPI IDs / Virtual Payment Addresses provided by organizers to facilitate peer-to-peer settlement outside the app. (Note: We do not process banking transactions, store credit cards, or collect financial credentials.)

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, crash reports, and network connectivity state.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID) and Apple IDFA for non-personalized and personalized advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Facilitate recreational sports match scheduling, RSVPs, waitlists, and attendance tracking.
  • Generate formatted WhatsApp roll-call lists and payment settlement summaries upon user request.
  • Calculate fair per-head costs and manage equipment kitty balances.
  • Display in-app advertisements (banner, interstitial, rewarded) via Google Mobile Ads.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising Mediation

We work with trusted third-party providers:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication and Cloud Firestore database (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob: Primary ad delivery network (AdMob Privacy).
  • Unity Ads (Mediation Adapter): Ad network partner (Unity Privacy).
  • Liftoff Monetize / Vungle (Mediation Adapter): Ad network partner (Liftoff Privacy).

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Information is only shared within your match groups (e.g., rosters shown to teammates) or when legally required by law. All data is protected using industry-standard Google Cloud Firebase security protocols.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
